    Permissions ADMIN

    Choose what each person can see and do. Their role gives a starting set; switch features on or off for the person on top of it. Admins always have everything. A change applies to the person's very next action, and they see new or removed tabs the next time they open or refresh the app.

    Permissions need a one-time database update. Run run-in-supabase-5-permissions.sql in Supabase → SQL Editor, then reload this page. Until then everyone keeps what their role gives them.

    Choose a person on the left to see and change what they can do.

    Renewal reminders ADMIN

    The Owner and everyone allowed to “see renewals” get a reminder in the bell for every renewal that is coming up. Choose when the reminders start and how often they repeat. They stop once the renewal is marked as renewed; overdue renewals keep reminding.

    Reminder settings need a one-time database update. Run run-in-supabase-9-renewals.sql in Supabase → SQL Editor, then reload this page. Until then the standard settings apply (start 7 days before, repeat every hour).
