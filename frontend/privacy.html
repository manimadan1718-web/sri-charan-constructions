Sri Charan Constructions SITE ACTIVITY LOG

Privacy Policy

Sri Charan Constructions · Site Activity Log app · Last updated 13 October 2026

This policy explains what the Sri Charan Constructions Site Activity Log app (the “App”) records, why, who can see it and how it is protected. The App is a private working tool for our own staff. It is not a public service and has no advertising.

Who uses the App

Only people whom an Administrator at Sri Charan Constructions has given an account — site supervisors, the owner and administrators. Nobody can sign up on their own. The App is not meant for children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

What the App records

AccountsYour name, your role, and your PIN. The PIN is stored only as a one-way scrambled value (a hash), so nobody — including administrators — can read it. We also keep who created, changed or removed an account, and when.
Site recordsWhat staff enter for each working day: date, site, vehicle or machine number, meter readings, diesel, loads (and where they were unloaded), operator name and remarks. Photos of meter readings and diesel slips are stored privately.
DocumentsFiles that the owner or an administrator uploads (for example work orders and tax invoices) with the name, type, site and notes typed alongside them.
PurchasesMaterial that arrives at a site: date, site, supplier, vehicle number, item, load weight, empty weight and the net weight worked out from them, an optional attached file (such as a weighbridge slip or invoice), and who entered it.
PaymentsEmployee name, days worked and amount for weekly (food) and monthly (salary) payments, and who entered them.
Activity logFor security and accountability: sign-ins (successful and failed), the actions people take, the time, and the IP address and type of browser or device used. PINs and tokens are never written to the log.
On your deviceA temporary sign-in token kept in the browser’s session storage (it is cleared when you sign out or close the App), and the App’s own pages, images and fonts saved so it opens quickly and can show an “offline” screen. No business records are stored on the device.

What the App does not do

  • It does not track your location, and does not read your contacts, messages or microphone.
  • It only sees a photo when you choose or take one inside the App.
  • It has no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no tracking cookies.
  • It does not load fonts, scripts or images from other companies’ websites.

How the information is used

To run site operations: keeping daily records, producing reports (Excel, PDF), keeping payment records, and keeping an audit trail of who did what. We do not sell the information or use it for advertising.

Who can see what

  • Supervisors can add site entries, purchases and payment lines, and see the purchases and payment lines they saved themselves.
  • The owner can see records, summaries, documents, payments and the activity log.
  • Administrators can see and manage everything, including users.

Who else handles the information

The App is hosted on Render and its database and file storage are provided by Supabase. They process the information on our behalf, only to run the App. We do not share it with anyone else, except where the law requires.

How it is protected

  • All traffic uses HTTPS.
  • PINs are hashed; sign-in attempts are rate-limited.
  • Every action is checked against the person’s role on the server, not just on screen.
  • Photos and documents sit in private storage and are opened only through short-lived links.
  • Important actions are written to the activity log.

How long it is kept, and deleting it

Records are kept for as long as Sri Charan Constructions needs them for its business and legal obligations. Administrators can delete users, entries, documents and payments. Activity-log entries are kept for audit and security. To ask for something to be corrected or deleted, contact us (below).

Your choices

You can ask what is recorded about you, ask for corrections, or ask for your account to be switched off. You can stop using the App at any time by signing out and uninstalling it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the App handles information we will update this page and its “last updated” date.

Contact

Questions or requests about this policy:

please contact the Administrator of your site, or write to Sri Charan Constructions.
